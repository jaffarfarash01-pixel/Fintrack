Financial Literacy

Tips built for the student life

🍱
Cook Your Own Meals

Preparing food at home can save ₹3,000–₹6,000/month compared to eating out daily.

🎯
The 50/30/20 Rule

Spend 50% on needs, 30% on wants, and save at least 20% of every stipend you receive.

📈
Start a Student SIP

₹500/month in a mutual fund SIP started now builds serious wealth through compounding over time.

🚌
Use Public Transport

A monthly bus/metro pass saves thousands compared to using daily ride-hailing apps.
